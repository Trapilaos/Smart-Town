How it is calculated

  • Lights switch on at full brightness 10 minutes before sunset.
  • From 00:30 to 05:00 local time they dim to 50%.
  • They switch off 10 minutes after sunrise.
  • Energy is estimated from the number of streetlights, their wattage, and the hours each one spends at each brightness.
